IIOU Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

IIOU is a personal IOU record-keeping application designed for Pioneers. We aim to collect only information needed to provide and secure the service.

Information we process

When you sign in, Pi Authentication provides an app-specific user identifier and, with your permission, your Pi username. We store these values so your account can be recognized and IOUs can be shared with the intended Pioneer.

For IOUs, we store the usernames of the two participants, amount in Pi, note, optional due date, status, timestamps, and a limited action history. We do not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, government identification, passwords, or wallet passphrases.

Pi payments

For Testnet payment validation and security, payment audit records may contain the Pi payment identifier, transaction identifier, app-specific Pi user identifier, username, amount, memo, network, direction, destination address, verification state, and timestamps. These records are used to confirm that the payment was actually completed and to prevent duplicate or conflicting processing. Your wallet passphrase is never requested or stored by IIOU.

How data is used

Data is used to authenticate users, display and synchronize IOUs between participants, prevent unauthorized changes, validate and audit Testnet payment flows, diagnose technical problems, and protect the integrity of the service.

Aggregate telemetry

We count daily pseudonymous users and aggregate events such as sign-ins, IOU creation, lifecycle actions, settlements and completed support payments. Pi user identifiers are transformed with a server-side keyed hash before counting. Telemetry does not contain IOU notes, counterparties or amounts and is visible only to the application administrator.

Sharing

An IOU is visible to its two participants. We do not sell personal data. Service infrastructure providers may process data only as necessary to host and operate IIOU.

Retention and deletion

Records may be retained while needed to provide a consistent ledger and for legitimate security, payment-audit, or technical purposes. A production deletion workflow will be defined before Mainnet launch, taking into account records that may need to be retained for integrity or security.

Security

Pi access tokens are verified server-side. Server API keys and wallet secrets are kept outside client code. Payment completion is checked against Pi Platform API state before it is treated as final. No system can guarantee absolute security, but IIOU is designed to minimize exposed data and privileges.

Important scope

IIOU is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, lender, escrow service, money transmitter, or dispute-resolution service.
