IIOU Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

IIOU is a personal IOU record-keeping application designed for Pioneers. We aim to collect only information needed to provide the service.

Information we process

When you sign in, Pi Authentication provides an app-specific user identifier and, with your permission, your Pi username. We store these values so your account can be recognized and IOUs can be shared with the intended Pioneer.

For IOUs, we store the usernames of the two participants, amount in Pi, note, optional due date, status, timestamps, and a limited action history. We do not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, government identification, passwords, or wallet passphrases.

Pi payments

Testnet payment records may contain Pi payment identifiers, transaction identifiers, amounts, memos, and payment status as required to validate the Pi payment flow. Your wallet passphrase is never requested or stored by IIOU.

How data is used

Data is used to authenticate users, display and synchronize IOUs between participants, prevent unauthorized changes, operate Testnet payment checks, diagnose technical problems, and protect the integrity of the service.

Sharing

An IOU is visible to its two participants. We do not sell personal data. Service infrastructure providers may process data only as necessary to host and operate IIOU.

Retention and deletion

Records may be retained while needed to provide a consistent ledger and for legitimate security or technical purposes. A production deletion workflow will be made available before Mainnet launch.

Security

Pi access tokens are verified server-side. Server API keys and wallet secrets are kept outside client code. No system can guarantee absolute security, but IIOU is designed to minimize exposed data and privileges.

Important scope

IIOU is a record-keeping tool. It is not a bank, lender, escrow service, money transmitter, or dispute-resolution service.
