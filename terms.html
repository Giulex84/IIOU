IIOU Terms of Service

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Purpose

IIOU helps Pioneers create and maintain shared personal records of amounts they believe are owed between them. It is a record-keeping and coordination tool, not a financial institution.

2. User responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy and legality of IOUs you create, accept, mark as paid, or confirm as settled. Do not create misleading, fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful records.

3. No lending, custody, or arbitration

IIOU does not itself lend Pi, set interest, guarantee repayment, hold funds in escrow, collect debts, determine whether a debt is legally enforceable, or resolve disputes between users. An IOU status reflects actions taken by participants in the app and is not a legal judgment.

4. Pi Network integration

IIOU uses Pi Authentication and may use Pi Testnet payment functionality for integration testing. The optional 0.1 Test-Pi support payment is not required to create, manage, or settle an IOU record. Payment processing is validated server-side against Pi Platform API data. Blockchain transactions can be irreversible, so always review payment details before confirming them in the Pi Wallet interface. IIOU will never ask you to provide your wallet passphrase inside the app.

5. Testnet

The current IIOU application is a Testnet product under active development. Testnet Pi has no Mainnet value. Features, data structures, and availability may change as the product is improved before any separate Mainnet release.

6. Availability and changes

We may modify, suspend, or discontinue features when needed for security, reliability, compliance, or product development. We aim to preserve record integrity but cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation.

7. Limitation

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, IIOU is provided without guarantees that every user-entered claim is accurate or that another user will repay an IOU. Users should not rely on IIOU as a substitute for professional legal or financial advice.
